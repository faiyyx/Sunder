Gold / Forex / CFDs (lots)

        

          

          

        

        

          

          

        

        

        
Most brokers: gold 1 lot = 100 oz, so a $1 move = $100 per lot. Sizes are always rounded down to the lot step so you never risk more than planned.

      


      

        
Crypto (USDT)

        

          

          

        

        
        
The USDT amount is the position notional (what you buy). With leverage, the margin you post is notional ÷ leverage — the risk at the stop is the same either way.
